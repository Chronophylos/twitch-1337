game: Red Dead Redemption 2
Side activities checklist

Dinosaur bones, Rock carvings and Dreamcatchers are collectible items around the map. Tarot cards give bonuses to core stats. Dueling and bounty hunting are available in each town. Fishing contests and the Hot air balloon ride are in Rhodes. Gold bars sell at the Fence for $500. Tips for finding Treasure map pieces and unlocking Gunslinger Outfit.
