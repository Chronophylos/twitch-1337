Easy sourdough bread for beginners

Mix 500 g flour, 350 g water, 100 g starter and 10 g salt. Let it rest for 30 minutes, then stretch and fold every half hour for 2 hours. Shape, proof overnight in the fridge and bake in a Dutch oven at 240 C for 20 minutes covered and 20 minutes uncovered. Comments (42): Mine came out flat, any tips?
