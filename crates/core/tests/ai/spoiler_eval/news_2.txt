Take-Two earnings call: GTA 6 release window confirmed, investors react

Take-Two Interactive reaffirmed the release window during its quarterly earnings call. CEO Strauss Zelnick said the company remains "highly confident" in the date and expects record net bookings. Shares rose 6% in after-hours trading. Analysts expect the title to sell over 25 million copies in its first month.
