game: The Last of Us Part II
Accessibility and combat tips

The game offers over 60 accessibility options. Crafting: gather scissors and rags to craft shivs; use stealth by crouching in tall grass and listening mode to detect enemies through walls. Upgrade weapons at workbenches using parts. Best skill upgrades: Crafting speed, Listen Mode range. Recommended difficulty: Moderate for first run. New Game Plus unlocks all weapons at max level.
