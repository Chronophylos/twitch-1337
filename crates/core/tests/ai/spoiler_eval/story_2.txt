game: Elden Ring
Elden Ring Wiki - Endings

Warning: this page contains major plot spoilers.

At the end of the game, the Tarnished reaches the Elden Throne and is confronted by Radagon and the Elden Beast. After defeating the Elden Beast, the player is offered the choice to become Elden Lord and fix the Golden Order, or follow Ranni's path to the Age of Stars. Ranni reveals that she murdered Godwyn through the Night of the Black Knives, and Marika is revealed to have shattered the Elden Ring herself, because she was both Radagon and Marika in one body.
