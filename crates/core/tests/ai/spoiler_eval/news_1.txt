Insider Gaming: South Korea came close to an outright refusal to rate GTA VI due to "far too much genital exposure"
The country's regulators reportedly postponed their decision twice, with the primary concern being what was described as "out-of-context genital nudity."
During the first review on September 10, the committee was split evenly, with three votes for an Adults Only rating and three for Refusal. It wasn't until a third meeting that a nine-member committee voted 8-1 in favour of an Adults Only rating.
