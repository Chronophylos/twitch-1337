game: Red Dead Redemption 2
Beginner's guide: Hunting, fishing and horse bonding

Tips for new players: Bond with your horse by brushing and feeding it carrots; at level 4 you can use the speed boost. Hunt deer with the Varmint Rifle; use the Bolt Action for larger animals. Fishing: use a lure matching the water, and hold the bait when the fish bites. Poker and Blackjack are in Saint Denis. Upgrade your camp at the ledger. Open the map to find Legendary animals and Gang hideouts.
