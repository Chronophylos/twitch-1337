Opinion: Elden Ring's difficulty debate misses the point

Every few months the internet reignites the argument over whether FromSoftware should add an easy mode. I think both sides are talking past each other. Accessibility is not the same thing as difficulty, and the studio has always let players change the challenge through summons and builds. Here's why I think the debate keeps coming back, and what other studios could learn from it.
